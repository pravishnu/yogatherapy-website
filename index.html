Research & Insights

The Science Behind Therapeutic Yoga

Cyclic Meditation Research

S-VYASA studies show CM produces deeper relaxation than simple rest. EEG studies show increased alpha activity indicating calm awareness. CM reduces anxiety scores by 30-40% in controlled trials.

Sound & Nervous System

MSRT uses sound vibration to stimulate the vagus nerve. Chanting reduces blood pressure, heart rate, and cortisol. Resonance enhances parasympathetic dominance and reduces mental agitation.

Breathing & Stress Response

Slow breathing (5-6 breaths/min) increases vagal tone and HRV — key markers of stress resilience. SMET combines breathing, awareness, and relaxation for self-regulation.

Mind Energy & Focus

MEMT directs mental energy through guided awareness. Studies show significant improvement in attention tasks and reduced overthinking after 4 weeks of practice.
